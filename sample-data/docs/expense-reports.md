# Expense Reports

## What you can expense

Business travel, client meals, software approved by IT, and home-office items under the stipend (see Remote Work Policy).

## Limits

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meals while travelling | **$75** per day |
| Client dinner | $120 per person, with a receipt and attendee names |
| Taxi or rideshare | Reasonable fares; no limit, but economy class only |
| Hotel | Up to $220 per night in major cities |

Any single expense over **$500** needs written manager approval *before* you buy.

## How to submit

1. Open https://expenses.acme-onboarding.example and choose **New report**.
2. Photograph or upload each receipt. Receipts are required for anything over $25.
3. Pick a cost centre code (for example `ENG-100` for Engineering).
4. Submit within **30 days** of the purchase. Late reports may be rejected.

## Reimbursement

Approved reports are paid on the next Friday payroll run, usually within 5 business days.

## Not reimbursable

Alcohol (except client dinners with approval), personal travel extensions, fines and parking tickets.
