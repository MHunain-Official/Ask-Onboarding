INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCEDURE
===========================

Severity levels
---------------
SEV1  Customer-facing outage or confirmed data breach.
SEV2  Major feature degraded for many customers.
SEV3  Minor issue with a workaround.

Roles
-----
Incident Commander (IC)  Runs the response, makes decisions, does not debug.
Communications Lead      Posts updates every 30 minutes for SEV1 and every 60 minutes for SEV2.
Scribe                   Records the timeline in the incident channel.

Steps
-----
1. Declare the incident in Slack with: /incident declare sev1 "short title"
2. The bot creates the channel inc-YYYYMMDD-title and pages the IC.
3. Post the status page update at https://status.acme-onboarding.example.
4. Mitigate. Roll back first if a recent deploy is the likely cause.
5. After resolution, schedule a blameless post-mortem within 5 business days.

Data breach
-----------
If personal data may have been exposed, notify security@acme-onboarding.example
immediately. Legal must decide on customer notification within 72 hours.

Post-mortem template
--------------------
Summary, impact, timeline, root cause, what went well, what went badly, action items with owners.
