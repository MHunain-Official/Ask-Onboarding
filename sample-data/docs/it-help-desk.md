# IT Help Desk Guide

## How to get help

- Slack: `#help-it` (fastest for simple questions).
- Email: helpdesk@acme-onboarding.example.
- Phone: extension 4357 from an office phone, or +1-555-0100 externally.
- Portal: https://it.acme-onboarding.example

## Hours

Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Eastern Time. Urgent outages outside these hours go to the on-call engineer.

## Service levels

| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Nobody can log in | 15 minutes |
| P2 | One team blocked | 2 hours |
| P3 | Single user, workaround exists | 1 business day |

## Common requests

- **Password reset:** https://it.acme-onboarding.example/reset. Self-service, requires MFA.
- **New software:** request through the portal. Approval takes up to 3 business days.
- **Shared mailbox or group:** ask your manager to file the request.
- **Monitor or keyboard:** order from the equipment catalogue, up to $300 without approval.

## Tip

Include your asset tag (printed on the bottom of the laptop) in every hardware ticket.
