# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid by default

Employees who live within 50 miles of an office are expected in the office **2 days per week**: Tuesday and Thursday. Remote-first roles are marked in the job offer.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to **20 working days per year** with manager approval. Tell people-ops at least 30 days before you go because of tax and legal checks.

## Home-office stipend

- One-time setup stipend: **$600** for a desk, chair or monitor.
- Monthly internet stipend: **$40**.
- Submit through an expense report (see Expense Reports) with category `HOME`.

## Security when remote

- Use the VPN for internal tools.
- Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Keep company devices away from shared family use.

## Core hours

Be reachable between 11:00 and 15:00 Eastern Time on working days.
