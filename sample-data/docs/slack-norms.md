# Slack Norms

## Channels

- `#general`: company announcements only. Keep chatter out.
- `#help-it`: IT questions. The help desk replies within 4 business hours.
- `#eng-oncall`: pages and incident coordination.
- `#random`: memes, pets, lunch.
- Name team channels `team-<name>` and project channels `proj-<name>`.

## Etiquette

- Use threads. Reply in the thread instead of the main channel.
- Do not use `@channel` or `@here` outside of incidents. Use `@here` only when something needs attention in the next hour.
- Write the whole request in one message instead of just "Hi".
- Do not expect replies outside working hours. Set your status when you are away.

## Response times

| Type | Expected reply |
|---|---|
| Direct message | within 1 business day |
| Team channel | within 1 business day |
| `#eng-oncall` page | within 5 minutes |

## Sensitive data

Never paste passwords, API keys, customer personal data or salary details into Slack. Messages are retained for 2 years.
