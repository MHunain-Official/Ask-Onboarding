## Visitors

Register visitors at least 1 day before through the front-desk form. Visitors must wear a badge and be escorted at all times.

## Badges

Badges are issued on your first day by reception. A lost badge costs $15 to replace; report it to facilities@acme-onboarding.example straight away.

## Desk booking

Desks are booked through the Workplace app. Bookings are released at 09:30 if you have not checked in.
