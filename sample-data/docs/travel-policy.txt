TRAVEL POLICY
=============

Booking
-------
Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at https://travel.acme-onboarding.example.
Book flights at least 14 days ahead where possible.

Flights
-------
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more with manager approval.
- Choose the lowest logical fare. Refundable fares are only needed if plans may change.

Hotels
------
- Up to $220 per night in major cities (New York, London, San Francisco) and $160 elsewhere.
- Stay in a hotel from the approved list when one is available.

Ground transport
----------------
Use public transport or rideshare. Rental cars need approval, and you must decline the optional insurance because the company policy covers it.

Per diem
--------
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day. See Expense Reports.

Emergencies
-----------
Call the travel assistance line at +1-555-0166 (24/7). Tell your manager as soon as you can.

Personal days
-------------
You can add personal days to a trip, but you pay for the extra hotel nights and flight differences.
