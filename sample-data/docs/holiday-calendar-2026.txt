COMPANY HOLIDAY CALENDAR 2026
=============================

The company observes 11 paid holidays in 2026. They do not count against PTO.

Thu  Jan 1    New Year's Day
Mon  Jan 19   Martin Luther King Jr. Day (US offices)
Mon  May 25   Memorial Day (US offices)
Fri  Jul 3    Independence Day (observed, US offices)
Mon  Sep 7    Labor Day (US offices)
Thu  Nov 26   Thanksgiving (US offices)
Fri  Nov 27   Day after Thanksgiving
Fri  Dec 25   Christmas Day
Mon  Dec 28   Winter break day
Tue  Dec 29   Winter break day
Wed  Dec 30   Winter break day

Notes
-----
- Toronto and London offices follow local public holidays in place of the US-only dates.
  See people-ops for the local list.
- If you must work on a holiday (for example, on-call), you receive a day of
  time off in lieu within 60 days.
- The production deploy freeze runs from December 20 to January 3.
