# Meeting Norms

## Before the meeting

- Every meeting needs an agenda in the invite. No agenda, no meeting.
- Default length is **25 minutes** or **50 minutes**, to leave a break between meetings.
- Invite only the people who need to decide or contribute. Others can read the notes.

## During the meeting

- Start on time. Do not wait more than 3 minutes for latecomers.
- Cameras are encouraged but optional.
- Assign a note taker. Post notes in the team channel within 24 hours.

## No-meeting time

Wednesdays are meeting-free for engineering. Company-wide all-hands are on the first Thursday of each month at 11:00 Eastern Time.

## Time zones

Schedule meetings between 10:00 and 16:00 Eastern Time when attendees span Austin, Toronto and London. Record important meetings so that others can watch later.

## Decisions

Write decisions down in a short decision record with the owner, the date and the reason.
