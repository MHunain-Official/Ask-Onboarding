# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2023
**Last reviewed:** March 14, 2023

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees receive **15 days** of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at 1.25 days per month.

## Carry-over

You may carry over a maximum of **5 days** into the next year. Unused days above that limit expire on December 31.

## Requesting time off

- Submit requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for trips longer than 3 days.
- Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. See the benefits overview for details.
