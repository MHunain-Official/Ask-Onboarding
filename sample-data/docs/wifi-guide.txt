WIFI GUIDE
==========

Networks
--------
AcmeCorp      Employee network. Sign in with your company username and password (802.1X).
AcmeGuest     Visitors only. The access code changes every Monday and is available at reception.
AcmeIoT       Printers and devices. Registered devices only.

Connecting to AcmeCorp
----------------------
1. Select "AcmeCorp" from the Wi-Fi list.
2. Username: your full email address.
3. Password: your company password. Accept the certificate issued by "Acme Root CA".
4. Your device connects automatically in the future.

Wifi password policy
--------------------
- There is no shared password for AcmeCorp. Never ask a colleague for one.
- Guest codes expire after 7 days. Do not give the guest code to anyone who is not a visitor.
- Do not connect personal devices to AcmeCorp. Personal phones may use AcmeGuest.
- Do not run your own access point or hotspot in the office.

Speeds and limits
-----------------
AcmeCorp supports Wi-Fi 6 (5 GHz). Guest network speed is limited to 25 Mbps per device.

Problems?
---------
Forget the network, reconnect, and try again. If it still fails, post in #help-it
with your device model and the room you are in.
