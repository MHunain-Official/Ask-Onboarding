# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective date:** July 1, 2025
**Last reviewed:** June 2, 2025

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees receive **20 days** of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at about 1.67 days per month. Employees with 5 or more years of service receive 25 days.

## Carry-over

You may carry over a maximum of **10 days** into the next year. Unused days above that limit expire on March 31 of the following year.

## Requesting time off

- Submit requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for trips longer than 5 days.
- Your manager approves or declines within 2 business days.
- Requests for the last week of December must be submitted by November 15.

## Company holidays

Company holidays do not count against your PTO. See the 2026 holiday calendar.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not need advance approval. See the benefits overview.
