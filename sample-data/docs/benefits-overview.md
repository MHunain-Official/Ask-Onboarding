# Benefits Overview

Benefits start on the **first day of the month after your start date**.

## Health

- Medical, dental and vision are offered through CareWell. The company pays 90% of the employee premium and 70% of dependants.
- Enrol within **30 days** of becoming eligible, or wait for open enrolment in November.

## Retirement

The company matches 401(k) contributions up to **4%** of salary. Matching vests immediately.

## Leave

- Sick leave: 10 paid days per year, separate from PTO.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver, 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
- Bereavement: 5 days for immediate family.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a **$1,000** annual learning budget for books, courses and conferences. Submit it through an expense report with category `LEARN`.

## Wellness

A $50 per month wellness stipend is paid with payroll. The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) is free and confidential at 1-800-555-0177.
